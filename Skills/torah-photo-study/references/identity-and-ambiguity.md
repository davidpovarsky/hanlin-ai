# Identity and Ambiguity Guidelines

1. **Quoted Work vs. Physical Book**: When an excerpt opens with quotation markers such as "וכתב הרמב״ם" or "איתא בגמרא", the quoted text belongs to the cited work, but the physical printed book is the host work. Report the citation clearly and do not conflate the two.
2. **Canonical Location vs. Edition**: An internal Talmud folio (e.g. Berakhot 2a) does not imply a specific printed page number in a modern edition. Do not fabricate edition page numbers.
3. **Common Liturgical & Biblical Texts**: Well-known verses (e.g. "ואהבת לרעך כמוך") and prayers appear across hundreds of titles. Report multiple candidates and note the common nature of the passage.
