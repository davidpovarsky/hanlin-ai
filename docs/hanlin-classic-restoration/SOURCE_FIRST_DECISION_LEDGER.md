## 1. Governing Principles

1. **Original Chat Authority (`478cdd2c`):** `ChatView.swift`, `ChatBubbleView` in `ChatViewComponents.swift`, `ListView.swift`, and `ChatandFile.swift` are restored from the historical implementation as the single authoritative user/assistant chat surface.
2. **Deliberate Composer Exception:** The native SwiftChat-inspired multiline Liquid Glass composer in `ChatViewBottom.swift` is retained (`KEEP_APPROVED_COMPOSER`), with its cramped internal `bottomStatusRow` removed from the primary input capsule and redesigned into a clean, non-overlapping accessory/header layout that fits Hebrew RTL, English LTR, and iPad multitasking.
3. **Downstream Presentation & Agent Activity as Adapters:** `AgentRunTranscriptView`, `AgentActivitySummaryView`, `ChatEmbeddedResultHost`, and `AgentEvidenceSummaryView` operate strictly as subordinate adapters inside `ChatBubbleView`—never replacing `ChatBubbleView`, never owning the final assistant answer, and never exposing raw private chain-of-thought.
