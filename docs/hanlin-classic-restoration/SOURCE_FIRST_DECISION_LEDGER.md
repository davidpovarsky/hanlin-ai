## 1. Governing Principles

1. **Original Chat Authority (`478cdd2c23ae2b1a52044cff52ce720830128ea2`):** `AI_HLY/ChatView.swift`, `ChatBubbleView` in `AI_HLY/Views/Components/ChatViewComponents.swift`, `AI_HLY/ListView.swift`, and `AI_HLY/ChatandFile.swift` are restored from the historical implementation as the single authoritative user/assistant chat surface.
2. **Deliberate Composer Exception (`KEEP_APPROVED_COMPOSER`):** The native SwiftChat-inspired multiline Liquid Glass composer in `AI_HLY/Views/Components/ChatViewBottom.swift` is retained, with its cramped internal `bottomStatusRow` removed from inside the primary input glass capsule and placed in an external `composerAccessoryBar` above the capsule (`hanlin-model-selector` + `hanlin-active-capabilities-pill`) that works across English LTR, Hebrew RTL, Chinese, and iPad Split View.
